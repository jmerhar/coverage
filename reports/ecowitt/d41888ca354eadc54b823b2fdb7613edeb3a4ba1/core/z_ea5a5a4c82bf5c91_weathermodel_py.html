1"""A weather model's view of each station's location, as the collector stores it. 

2 

3The collector fetches Open-Meteo's 15-minute values for every station with coordinates and 

4writes them to the `model` table beside the station's own readings, so the two can be compared 

5and the model can supply what a station does not measure (cloud cover, the weather code). 

6 

7Rows are tagged `station`, `source` (`open_meteo`) and `model` (the model asked for), so 

8choosing another model starts new series rather than putting a step in the old ones. Field names 

9follow the station's tables -- `temp_c`, `speed_kmh`, `dir_deg` -- in the operator's units, and 

10each row's time is when its values are valid, not when they were fetched. 

11""" 

12
