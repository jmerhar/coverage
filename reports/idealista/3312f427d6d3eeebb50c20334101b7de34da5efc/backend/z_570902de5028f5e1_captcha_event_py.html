1"""CaptchaEvent model — one row per captcha / protection encounter. 

2 

3Each row records a single point where the scraper met a DataDome challenge (or 

4an outright block), how it was classified, how it was resolved, and — for 

5escalated ones — how long the scraper backed off. This is the query surface for 

6the captcha dashboard. 

7 

8Populated two ways, mirroring ``deploy_events``: 

9 

10* a one-time historical **backfill** from ``scraper.log`` cross-referenced with 

11 ``captcha_sessions`` / ``scrape_runs`` (``source="backfill"``, see 

12 ``scraper/captcha_backfill.py``), and 

13* **live** at the moment each challenge is handled going forward 

14 (``source="live"``, via ``services.captcha_events.record_captcha_event``). 

15 

16The two never double-count: both compute the same ``dedup_key`` and inserts 

17ignore unique-key conflicts. 

18""" 

19

28class CaptchaEvent(Base): 

29 """A single captcha / protection encounter, classified and resolved. 

30 

31 ``kind`` is the headline category shown on the dashboard: 

32 

33 * ``auto`` — CloakBrowser auto-resolved the DataDome challenge (only ever 

34 visible in logs, never escalated). 

35 * ``slider`` — a ``t=fe`` challenge that did not auto-resolve and was 

36 escalated to a manual Telegram solve. 

37 * ``hard_block`` — a ``t=bv`` unsolvable "ban view" hard block. 

38 * ``block`` — a plain "you have been blocked" / 403 page (``BlockedError``). 

39 * ``unknown`` — could not be classified. 

40 

41 "Pages scraped since the last block" (the dashboard's work-before-captcha 

42 chart) is derived at query time from ``scrape_runs`` page counters, not stored 

43 here — see ``api/routers/captcha_metrics.build_pages_since_block``. 

44 """
